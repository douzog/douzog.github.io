About Me

Isabella is an interdisciplinary scientist, biophysics PhD student, and founder. She also Co-Hosts Æthos-Sundai hacks in Berlin. Her expertise in computer science and machine learning led her to several research projects, from exploring organic molecules in space at ESA to designing Bayesian inference and deep learning algorithms at the University of Amsterdam's IMCN research group, and quantitative neuroscience at the Max Delbrück Center for Molecular Medicine and Charité University of Medicine in Berlin. Isabella's journey underscores that curiosity and perseverance fuel transformative innovation, and hopes to disrupt the photonics and AI infrastructure market with her startup.
